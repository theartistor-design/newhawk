Reduce Transaction Fees & Boost Customer Experience

In a digital-first economy, UK businesses cannot afford slow checkout lanes or high transaction processing rates. Every tap or insert on a card terminal counts toward your operating margin. Standard bank-issued terminals often come locked with complex lease rules and hidden interchange costs.

Hawk Talk Solutions Ltd connects you with modern merchant processing hardware tailored for high performance. Our chip and pin machine and contactless card reader systems feature fast Wi-Fi, 4G, and Bluetooth options to let you handle payments anywhere on your shop floor. By analyzing your monthly card statements, we identify merchant account savings to secure highly competitive transaction rates.

Best of all, we set up systems to support next-day settlement, meaning you don't have to wait several banking days to access your funds. The changeover process is entirely handled by us, ensuring a clean setup with absolutely zero trading disruption for your store.
