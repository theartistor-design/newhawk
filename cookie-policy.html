Cookie Policy

Last updated: July 19, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how Hawk Talk Solutions Ltd uses cookies and similar technologies when you visit our website.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device to store data that can be recalled by a web server in the domain that placed the cookie. We use cookies to respect your preferences and improve web usability.

2. Types of Cookies We Use

Our website is designed to protect your privacy and uses minimal cookies. Specifically, we only use:

  • Strictly Necessary Cookies: These are functional cookies essential for you to browse our site, navigate between pages, and submit the contact form securely. Without these cookies, basic site functions would not work properly.

3. Third-Party Cookies

We do not run third-party advertising cookies or trackers on our site. If we integrate web analytics (like Google Analytics) in the future to improve user experience, we will update this policy and request your consent prior to activation.

4. How to Control Cookies

Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings preferences. You can configure your browser to block or delete cookies, but doing so may limit your ability to use certain interactive form options on our site.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding our use of cookies, please contact our support team at sales@hawktalksolutions.co.uk.
